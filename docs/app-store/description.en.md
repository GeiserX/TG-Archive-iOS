## Description

TG Archive is the iOS client for the open-source Telegram-Archive server you run yourself. Enter your server's address, sign in with a viewer account or paste a share link, and read your backed-up chats natively on your iPhone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Browse your chat list with previews, folders, archived chats and forum topics.
- Read every thread the way the archive kept it: text formatting, replies, forwards, reactions, polls, locations, contacts, stickers, pinned messages, and marks on messages that were edited or deleted after they were saved.
- Open photos full screen with zoom, play videos, round videos, voice notes and audio, and read voice note transcripts.
- Open documents in Quick Look and share files, when your login allows downloads.
- Search every message you can see and jump to the hit inside its chat.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- The app talks only to the server you enter. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.
- No accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your session is kept in the iOS Keychain on this device. Signing out ends it on your server and clears the cache.
- Read-only: the app never changes your archive.

WHAT YOU NEED
A Telegram-Archive server you run yourself, reachable over https (or plain http on your local network), with a viewer account or a share link. Setup guide: https://github.com/GeiserX/Telegram-Archive

OPEN SOURCE
TG Archive is free software under the GPL. The source code is at https://github.com/GeiserX/TG-Archive-iOS

TG Archive is part of the open-source Telegram-Archive project and is independent of Telegram; the server you run uses the Telegram API.
